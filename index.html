    Path of Crondis & Zebak

    Crondis puzzle agility

    • Fill the container at the waterfall, water the palm tree before the timer empties.
    • Zig-zag past crocodiles — getting caught spills water.
    • Run energy room — save stamina sips for the boss.

    Zebak ranged

    • Blood clouds heal him — kill them the moment they spawn.
    • Roar wave: jump with the rocks or eat the stun; acid trail blocks tiles.
    • Boulders push you — don't get shoved into acid.
    • Enrage: faster waves + more acid. Save a salt refresh for it.
    Path of Scabaras & Kephri

    Scabaras puzzle brain

    • Addition puzzle: stand on tiles summing to the target number.
    • Matching: flip and pair the symbols — memorize before flipping.
    • Fastest path room in the raid — bank the time here.

    Kephri melee (stab)

    • Shield up = DPS the exploding scarabs into her phase; dung attack arcs — move off the splat tile.
    • Swarm phase: Protect from Melee, step away from exploders.
    • Arcane scarabs heal her shield — kill them first every wave.
    • Fireball barrage: keep moving diagonally; never stand still.
    Path of Apmeken & Ba-Ba

    Apmeken puzzle chaos

    • Pillar room: match the sense (sight/smell/hearing) to the correct pillar.
    • Volatile monkeys explode — off-tile before the bar fills; cursed ones drain prayer.
    • Solo: do the vents in a fixed order and keep moving.

    Ba-Ba melee

    • Boulder roll: stand in the gap in the boulder line.
    • Sarcophagus slam drops rubble — don't attack through falling rocks.
    • Baboon waves: kill smalls fast (shadow baboons drain prayer, volatile explode).
    • Edge shove attack: stand off the pit line when she winds up.
    Path of Het & Akkha

    Het puzzle mirrors

    • Pick up the shield first — orb beam chunks you without it.
    • Rotate mirrors to route the light beam to the statue; mine rubble in the way.
    • Drop the shield before leaving — it's room-only.

    Akkha magic

    • Match protection prayer to his style — he swaps after every special.
    • Quadrant specials: hug the current quadrant edge, sidestep the elemental orbs.
    • Memory special: repeat the flashed sequence — walk it calmly, it's not a DPS race.
    • Final phase: detonating orbs sweep the room — count lanes before committing.
    The Wardens

    P1 — Obelisk all styles

    • DPS the obelisk; dodge the skull volleys and lightning tiles.
    • Prayer-switch on the warden attacks from the sides.
    • This phase sets your P2 — bank damage safely, don't greed through skulls.

    P2 — Core burst dps

    • Warden slams → core exposed → dump your biggest hits into it.
    • Between cores: dodge vents and lightning, keep supplies topped.
    • Missing a core window is the raid's biggest time loss.

    P3 — Enrage survive

    • Rising floor tiles: pre-move, never react late.
    • Phantom bosses echo earlier rooms — same answers as before (Ba-Ba slam, Akkha orbs, Kephri swarm, Zebak wave).
    • Lightning intensifies as hp drops — save ambrosia / thralls / specs for the last 30%.
